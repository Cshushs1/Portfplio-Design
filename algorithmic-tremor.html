01 / Introduction

AI Anxiety &
Digital Identity

Algorithmic Tremor is an interactive installation centered on AI anxiety and the instability of identity within algorithmic environments. Through bodily interaction, the work translates human movement into shifting digital imagery, exploring how information overload, algorithmic discipline, and technological systems can reshape our perception of the self.

03 / Concept

Body As
Interface

AI Anxiety Identity Algorithm Body Interaction

The participant's body becomes an interface between physical movement and algorithmic transformation. Leap Motion captures gestures and movement, while TouchDesigner translates these actions into a changing visual environment. The image is no longer stable: interaction produces distortion, dissolution, and erosion, turning the digital image into a reflection of an increasingly unstable relationship between human agency and technological systems.
