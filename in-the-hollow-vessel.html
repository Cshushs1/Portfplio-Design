ENTER THE
SUBCONSCIOUS.

In the Hollow Vessel is a narrative puzzle game set within a mysterious 18th-century hotel, where the player takes the role of a hypnotist and host.

Each day, a new guest arrives troubled, silent, and unknowingly trapped within their own fractured dreams. Through top-down exploration, symbolic environments, and memory-based puzzles, the player enters their subconscious, navigates surreal dream-rooms, and uncovers emotional wounds hidden beneath layers of illusion.

01 / RESEARCH

HYPNOSIS,
CONTROL &
ILLUSION.

Hypnosis becomes both a narrative device and a system for shaping the player’s perception of reality.

The project explores cognitive bias, unreliable memory, suggestion, emotional projection, and perceptual dislocation to create psychological tension between control and illusion.

04 / WORLD

CHARACTER,
UI & WORLD.

The hotel connects individual characters, symbolic objects, interfaces, and recurring daily systems into one narrative space.

THE PERFUMER
Elegant, restrained, and perfectionist. The pursuit of pure order leads toward emotional numbness and self-destruction.
THE OFFICER
Disciplined, stoic, and bound by duty. Excessive obedience gradually erases individuality and produces internal collapse.
THE KING
Curious, intelligent, and narcissistic. Rational understanding becomes a cage as knowledge overlaps with loneliness.

06 / OUTCOME

FRAGMENTED
MEMORIES.

Moving through the hotel, the player encounters conversations that appear to belong to strangers but gradually begin to echo the self.

Through restrained conversations, players access the travelers’ hidden narratives. Each exchange surfaces fragmented memories distributed across the hotel’s spaces. What initially appears as dialogue with others gradually becomes a structured process of self-interpretation shaped by interaction and narrative design.
