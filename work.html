Send Tears to the
Lovesickness of the World

Moving Image / Video
2022
01 / OTHER WORKS

You become more and more bored. Shame mixed with strange laughter becomes pathetic—or perhaps it has always been this way, even as you try to avoid the tears.

But when you saw me, you were shocked. I could not tell whether you saw something strange or a terminal patient. You screamed, saying: “What a pity—a good face, at such a young age, suffering from a rare disease of suspicion in the history of mankind.”
